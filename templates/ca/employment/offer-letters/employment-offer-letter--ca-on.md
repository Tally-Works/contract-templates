# Employment Offer Letter

> **Drafting note:** An Ontario offer letter must be signed before the start date (the job itself is the consideration for its terms) and must not contract out of the *Employment Standards Act, 2000* (ESA). The termination clause in Section 9 is drafted to provide the ESA minimums and no less; clauses that could provide less than the ESA in any circumstance have been held void in Ontario, which leaves the employer liable for common-law reasonable notice. Have an Ontario employment lawyer review before use. Non-compete clauses are prohibited for most Ontario employees under the ESA (s. 67.2) and are not included.

[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]

[DATE]

**Private and confidential**

[CANDIDATE NAME]
[ADDRESS]

Dear [FIRST NAME],

**Re: Offer of employment**

We are pleased to offer you employment with [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] (the "Company") on the terms set out in this letter (the "Agreement"). Please review it carefully. We encourage you to obtain independent legal advice before you sign.

## 1. Position

Your position is **[JOB TITLE]**, reporting to the [MANAGER TITLE]. Your duties are described in Schedule A and may be reasonably modified by the Company from time to time. [This is a full-time position / This is a part-time position of [NUMBER] hours per week.]

## 2. Start date

Your employment will begin on **[START DATE]**. [This offer is conditional on satisfactory completion of the conditions in Section 12.]

## 3. Place of work

Your place of work is [the Company's office at [ADDRESS], Ontario / your home in Ontario, with attendance at the Company's office at [ADDRESS] as reasonably required]. You will be required to travel [occasionally / regularly] in the course of your duties.

## 4. Compensation

The Company will pay you an annual salary of **$[AMOUNT] CAD**, paid [bi-weekly / semi-monthly] by direct deposit, less required statutory deductions. Your salary will be reviewed [annually].

[You are eligible to participate in the Company's [bonus / commission plan], a copy of which is attached as Schedule B. Any bonus is discretionary and is subject to the terms of the plan, including the requirement that you be actively employed on the payment date, except where the ESA requires otherwise.]

## 5. Hours of work

Your regular hours of work are [NUMBER] hours per week, [Monday to Friday]. Your position is [subject to / exempt from] the overtime provisions of the ESA. [Any overtime must be approved in advance in writing and will be paid or banked in accordance with the ESA.]

## 6. Vacation

You are entitled to [three (3)] weeks of paid vacation per year, accrued in accordance with the Company's vacation policy, and in any event no less than the vacation time and vacation pay required by the ESA. Vacation is to be taken at times approved by your manager.

## 7. Benefits

Subject to the terms of the applicable plans and any waiting period, you are eligible to participate in the Company's group benefits plan, which currently includes [extended health, dental, life and disability insurance]. The Company may amend or replace its benefit plans from time to time. Benefit entitlements are governed by the plan documents and the insurer's terms.

## 8. Probationary period

The first [three (3)] months of your employment are a probationary period during which the Company will assess your suitability for the position. During the probationary period, the Company may end your employment [without notice or pay in lieu, except as required by the ESA / on the notice required by the ESA].

## 9. Termination of employment

9.1 **By you.** You may resign on [two (2)] weeks' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of the notice period, in which case it will pay you your base salary for the waived portion [and your benefits will continue for that period as required by the ESA].

9.2 **By the Company for cause.** The Company may terminate your employment at any time for wilful misconduct, disobedience or wilful neglect of duty that is not trivial and has not been condoned by the Company, without notice, pay in lieu or severance pay, as permitted by the ESA.

9.3 **By the Company without cause.** The Company may terminate your employment at any time without cause by providing you with only (a) the minimum notice of termination, or pay in lieu of notice, required by the ESA; (b) the minimum severance pay, if any, required by the ESA; and (c) continuation of your benefits for the minimum period required by the ESA. [In addition, the Company will provide [NUMBER] week(s) of base salary for each completed year of service, to a maximum of [NUMBER] weeks, inclusive of the ESA minimums, conditional on your signing a full and final release in the Company's standard form.] You agree that these entitlements are in full satisfaction of any claim you may have to notice, pay in lieu of notice, severance pay or damages arising from the termination of your employment, whether under statute, common law or otherwise.

9.4 **ESA compliance.** Nothing in this Agreement provides you with less than your minimum entitlements under the ESA. If any provision of this Agreement would, in any circumstance, provide you with less than those minimums, you will receive the ESA minimums in place of that provision.

9.5 **Return of property.** On termination for any reason, you will immediately return all Company property, documents and data in your possession.

## 10. Confidentiality and intellectual property

During and after your employment, you will keep the Company's confidential information confidential and use it only for the Company's benefit. All work product, inventions and intellectual property you create in the course of your employment belong to the Company, and you waive any moral rights in them. You agree to sign the Company's Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Agreement, attached as Schedule C, as a condition of employment.

## 11. Company policies

You agree to comply with the Company's policies as amended from time to time, including its workplace violence and harassment policy, health and safety policy, and code of conduct. If a policy conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement prevails.

## 12. Conditions of offer

This offer is conditional on: (a) proof that you are legally entitled to work in Canada; [(b) satisfactory reference checks;] [(c) a satisfactory criminal record check, where permitted by law and relevant to the position;] and (d) your signing Schedule C.

## 13. General

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the federal laws of Canada applicable in Ontario.

13.2 This Agreement, with its Schedules, is the entire agreement between you and the Company and replaces all prior discussions, offers and representations. It may be amended only in writing signed by both parties. If any provision is unenforceable, the rest continues in effect.

13.3 You acknowledge that you have read and understood this Agreement, that you have had the opportunity to obtain independent legal advice, and that you are signing it voluntarily before your first day of work.

Please confirm your acceptance by signing below and returning a copy to [NAME] by **[DEADLINE DATE]**. We look forward to welcoming you to the team.

Yours truly,

**[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]**

Name: [NAME]
Title: [TITLE]

**Acceptance**

I accept employment on the terms set out above.
