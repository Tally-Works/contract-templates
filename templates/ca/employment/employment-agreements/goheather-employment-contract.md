> **Build notes (goHeather Canada).** This is the document goHeather's Canadian employment contract builder produces. Fill-in fields are in square brackets, in capitals. Sections and sentences the builder includes only for some answers carry an italic build note naming the question. The questions the builder asks, in order: employer's legal name; employee's first and last name; start date; position; location type; optional custom paragraphs about the position; probationary period; term (indefinite, or fixed with an end date); full-time or part-time; regular hours, casual hours or neither; province; exclusivity; salary and whether it is annual or hourly; pay frequency; group benefits, other benefits and when benefits begin; bonus (fixed amount or percentage, frequency, fiscal year end); optional custom paragraphs about benefits; vacation weeks; notice of resignation; termination clause type (none, minimum statutory notice, or enhanced with a number of weeks); whether to include confidentiality, intellectual property and non-solicitation clauses; background check. Delete the build notes before use.

**EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT**

THIS AGREEMENT, between [EMPLOYER LEGAL NAME] (the "Employer") and [EMPLOYEE FIRST NAME] [EMPLOYEE LAST NAME] (the "Employee"), is effective as of [START DATE] (the "Effective Date");

WHEREAS the Employee wants to be employed by the Employer and the parties have agreed to enter into this Employment Contract ("Agreement").

**POSITION, TERM AND DUTIES**

1. The Employer agrees to employ the Employee and the Employee accepts employment with the Employer under the terms and conditions outlined in this Agreement. The Employee's position shall be [POSITION TITLE].

2. [WORK LOCATION SENTENCE] *[Build note (goHeather Canada): The builder writes this sentence from the "location type" answer, stating where the Employee will work: at the Employer's location, remotely, or a combination.]*

3. The Employer may require the Employee to work at the Employer's location or remotely, within reason and with notice, indefinitely, without breaching this Agreement.

*[Build note (goHeather Canada): If the user adds custom paragraphs about the position, each one is inserted here as its own numbered clause.]*

4. [PROBATIONARY PERIOD SENTENCE] During this probationary period, the Employee's employment may be terminated without notice. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include only if there is a probationary period. The builder writes the first sentence from the probation answer, stating the length of the probationary period from the Effective Date.]*

5. The Employee will begin employment on the Effective Date and employment shall continue indefinitely. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): Term = Indefinite. If Term = Fixed, replace "indefinitely." with the following:]* until a fixed end date of [FIXED END DATE]. The employment relationship automatically terminates on [FIXED END DATE] without any obligation of the parties to provide notice or payment in lieu of notice. If employment is terminated before [FIXED END DATE], the parties agree that the termination entitlements as set out below in the Termination section of this Agreement apply, not the balance of the fixed term.

6. The Employee agrees that the Employer has the right to change the Employee's job title, position, duties and/or place of employment in the same city, with notice, without any such change constituting a breach of this Agreement, a constructive dismissal, or a change in the rights and obligations of the parties under this Agreement.

7. The Employee agrees to provide their services and perform their duties faithfully and diligently to the best of their ability on a [FULL-TIME OR PART-TIME] basis, and to devote all their attention, skill and effort to such services and duties at all times in compliance with the policies, practices, directions, and instructions, written or oral, of the Employer.

8. The Employee agrees their regular hours of work shall be [REGULAR HOURS OF WORK]. The Employee further agrees that the Employer may require the Employee to work outside of their regular hours of work on occasion. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include only if the Employee has regular hours. If instead the Employee has casual hours, use the following clause; if neither, omit both:]* The Employee agrees their hours of work shall be irregular. The number of hours per week may vary. There could be zero hours of work in any given week and there could be a full set of hours in any given week.

9. The Employer may place the Employee on a temporary layoff in accordance with the [EMPLOYMENT STANDARDS LEGISLATION OF THE PROVINCE] as amended from time to time, without it being a constructive dismissal or a breach of this Agreement. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include once a province is chosen. The builder inserts the name of that province's employment standards statute, for example the Employment Standards Act, 2000 for Ontario.]*

10. The Employee agrees that their employment is on an exclusive basis. Notwithstanding the preceding, nothing in this Agreement shall preclude the Employee from public service endeavours or managing their investments outside standard work time. Moreover, the Employee shall be permitted to engage in other activities on behalf of another organization on an infrequent basis only, provided such activities are disclosed to the Employer, and the Employer agrees. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include only if the user wants an exclusivity clause.]*

**REMUNERATION**

11. The Employer shall pay the Employee a salary of [SALARY AMOUNT] per year, less applicable statutory withholdings and deductions. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): "per year" if the salary is annual; "per hour" if it is hourly.]*

12. The Employee's salary shall be paid on [PAY FREQUENCY]. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): The builder writes the pay frequency as a basis, for example "a bi-weekly basis".]*

13. The Employee is eligible for standard group benefits. The Employee is also eligible for: *[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include the first sentence only if the Employee gets group benefits. Include "The Employee is also eligible for:" and the list below only if other benefits are listed; if there are other benefits but no group benefits, the clause is just "The Employee is also eligible for:" and the list.]*
    - [OTHER BENEFIT] *[Build note (goHeather Canada): One bullet per other benefit entered.]*

14. The Employee's benefits will begin on the first day of employment. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include only if the Employee gets group benefits or other benefits. If benefits are delayed, the builder replaces "begin on the first day of employment." with a sentence stating when they begin, written from the benefits delay answer.]*

15. The Employee may be entitled to a bonus in the amount of $[BONUS AMOUNT] [BONUS FREQUENCY] based upon the attainment of targets to be determined by the Employer from time to time. All bonus amounts will be prorated based on the Employee's months of employment in the first fiscal year, and the Employer's fiscal year ends on [FISCAL YEAR END]. Any bonus which may be paid to the Employee is entirely at the discretion of the Employer, does not accrue, and is only earned and payable on the date that it is provided to the Employee by the Employer. In the event the Employee is terminated before the bonus is ordinarily paid because they have been guilty of willful misconduct, disobedience or willful neglect of duty that is not trivial and has not been condoned by the Employer, the Employee waives any claim to the bonus or any portion thereof. If the Employee's employment is terminated without cause, and the bonus would ordinarily be paid after the expiration of the statutory notice period, the Employee hereby waives any claim to that bonus or any portion thereof. If the Employee resigns before the date the bonus would ordinarily be paid, they forfeit any claim to the bonus or any portion thereof. The Employee also hereby waives any claim to constructive dismissal because the bonus is not paid, is less than was previously paid, or is less than is paid to another employee. The Employer may amend this bonus plan from time to time without breach of this Agreement. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include only if there is a bonus. If the bonus is a fixed amount, write "$[BONUS AMOUNT]"; if it is a percentage, write "[BONUS PERCENTAGE] percent of their base salary" instead. The bonus frequency is written as, for example, "per year".]*

*[Build note (goHeather Canada): If the user adds custom paragraphs about benefits, each one is inserted here as its own numbered clause.]*

**VACATION**

16. The Employee will be entitled to [VACATION WEEKS] weeks of paid vacation per year during the term of this Agreement, or as entitled by law, whichever is greater. Any unused vacation at the end of a calendar year, after the first year of employment, will not carry over to the next year. The times and dates for any vacation will be determined by mutual agreement between the Employer and the Employee.

**RESIGNATION**

*[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include this heading and clause only if the Employee must give notice of resignation. The number of weeks is written out in words.]*

17. The Employee may resign at any time by providing the Employer [RESIGNATION NOTICE WEEKS] week(s) notice of their resignation.

**TERMINATION**

*[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include this heading and clauses 18 to 19 unless the termination clause type is "No Termination Clause". The two types that include it are "Minimum Statutory Notice" and an enhanced clause with a number of weeks.]*

18. The Employer may terminate this Agreement at any time and will follow the [EMPLOYMENT STANDARDS LEGISLATION OF THE PROVINCE], as amended from time to time, by providing the Employee only with minimum notice of termination or termination pay in lieu of notice of termination and severance pay (if applicable) and benefit continuation (if applicable) and any other statutory standard in effect. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): "and severance pay (if applicable)" appears only when the province is Ontario. For "Minimum Statutory Notice", or an enhanced clause of 1 week, the clause ends here with a period. For an enhanced clause of more than 1 week, it ends with "; and" followed by paragraph (A):]*

    (A) The Employer shall also pay the Employee an additional amount equal to [NUMBER OF WEEKS] of base salary per year of completed service upon termination. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): The number of weeks shown is the termination notice weeks answer minus one, for example "1 week" for an answer of 2 and "3 weeks" for an answer of 4.]*

19. The Employee acknowledges, understands and agrees that the provision of the above termination of employment entitlements constitute full and final satisfaction of any claim or entitlement that the Employee may have against the Employer arising from or related to their employment's termination, whether according to statute, contract, common law or otherwise.

**NON-DISPARAGEMENT**

20. The Employee agrees that they shall not, both during and following the termination of their employment, make or publish or post written or oral statements on any medium which are damaging to the reputation of the Employer or any of its employees, customers, contractors, or affiliates.

**CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

*[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include this heading and clauses 21 to 23 only if the user wants a confidentiality clause.]*

21. The Employee acknowledges that the Employee will or may obtain or develop Confidential Information of the Employer, as defined in this section and that Confidential Information is the exclusive property of the Employer.

22. "Confidential Information" includes: All information relating to the management of the Employer, including but not limited to trade secrets, accounting records, work product, software, business operations plans, marketing, development, customer information, information that has been disclosed by a third party to the Employer that is protected by a non-disclosure or confidentiality agreement, and any other information reasonably held out or ought to be known as confidential.

23. The Employee will not disclose Confidential Information to any other person, otherwise than in the performance of their employment, unless compelled by law. The Employee agrees that if there is any question as to such disclosure, or if the Employee is compelled by law to disclose such Confidential Information, then the Employee will seek out senior management of the Employer before making any disclosure of the Employer's Confidential Information. The Employee agrees that, upon request of the Employer or upon the termination of this Agreement, the Employee will return to the Employer all Confidential Information in their possession or control.

**INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY**

*[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include this heading and clauses 24 to 25 only if the user wants an intellectual property clause.]*

24. All inventions, designs, ideas, works, creations, developments, programs, codes, drawings, sketches, compilations of information, analysis, experiments, data, formulas, methods, processes, techniques, prototypes, products, samples, equipment, tools and any modifications or improvements thereto (collectively, the "Intellectual Property") created by the Employee during the course of their employment with the Employer, whether at the Employer's place of business or otherwise, shall be, and remain, the exclusive property of the Employer and the Employee irrevocably and unconditionally acknowledges and agrees that they have no right, title or interest therein.

25. The Employee hereby assigns to the Employer any right, title, and interest to the Intellectual Property that they may have by having created, made, conceived, or contributed to any such Intellectual Property, either solely or with others, in whole or in part, in the course of their employment with the Employer.

**NON-SOLICITATION**

*[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include this heading and clauses 26 to 27 only if the user wants a non-solicitation clause.]*

26. Definitions: "Competitive Business" means any entity in the same business niche of the Employer within the Geographic Area; "Geographic Area" for the purposes of this Agreement is restricted to the 150km zone within the area of the Employer's location that the Employee works or would work but for them working remotely (if applicable).

27. The Employee understands and agrees that any attempt on the part of the Employee to induce current customers, reasonable customer leads (if applicable), employees and contractors to leave the Employer, or any effort by the Employee to interfere with the Employer's relationship with its current customers, reasonable customer leads (if applicable), employees and contractors would be damaging to the Employer. The Employee agrees that during the Employee's term of employment with the Employer and for twelve months after the end of that term, the Employee will not in any way, directly or indirectly:

    (a) Solicit, entice, or induce or attempt to solicit, entice, or induce any current customers, reasonable customer leads (if applicable), employees and contractors to leave the Employer for a Competitive Business in the Geographic Area.

**GENERAL**

28. This Agreement shall be governed in accordance with the laws of the Province of [PROVINCE]. The Employee consents to the jurisdiction of [PROVINCE] and of the Courts of [PROVINCE] for the purpose of any claims arising under this Agreement.

29. This Agreement may be executed either by original signature, or by electronic signature attached to this document, an e-mail, website, application, PDF, server, or by facsimile signature and may be executed by the parties in one or more counterparts, each of which when so executed and delivered, shall be an original and such counterparts shall together constitute one and the same agreement.

30. No amendment, modification or alteration of this Agreement shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by an authorized representative of both the Employee and the Employer.

31. The provisions of this Agreement, and any portion thereof, are severable; it being the intention of the parties that if for any reason, any such provision is held to be invalid, such invalidity shall not affect the remaining provisions, which shall remain in full force and effect as though such invalid provision had not been contained herein.

32. The Employer encourages the Employee to seek independent legal advice before signing this Agreement.

33. The Employee warrants to the Employer that there are no restrictive covenants which restrict them from performing their duties for the Employer, or that they have disclosed such restrictive covenants to the Employer, in connection with:

    (a) being employed by the Employer;

    (b) soliciting competitor clients or other third parties; and

    (c) using outside confidential information within their knowledge which may be useful in the performance of their duties for the Employer.

34. The Employee acknowledges that they have read and understood this Agreement and have been given the opportunity to seek independent legal advice concerning the terms contained in this Agreement.

35. The Employer may arrange for a background check that may include social media, work history, education, credit and a criminal record check on the Employee [collectively, "the Background Check"]. The Employee consents to a social media, work history, education, credit and criminal record check. The Employee further agrees to help the Employer obtain a criminal record check as a condition of employment. Lastly, if, within the first [BACKGROUND CHECK PERIOD] of employment, it is determined, in line with provincial human rights protections, that on the basis of reasonableness, the Employee failed the Background Check, the Employer may terminate the Employee's employment without notice. *[Build note (goHeather Canada): Include only if the user wants a background check. The builder writes the period from the province chosen.]*
