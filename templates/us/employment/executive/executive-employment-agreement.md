# Executive Employment Agreement

> **Drafting note:** This is a state-neutral, full-form agreement for a C-level or senior executive hire. Replace every `[BRACKETED]` placeholder, choose the bracketed alternatives that apply, and delete the drafting notes before use. Restrictive covenants (Section 9) are unenforceable or heavily restricted in several states, including California, Minnesota, North Dakota and Oklahoma; use the state-specific variant where one exists or take local advice.

This **Executive Employment Agreement** (the "Agreement") is made on [DATE] between:

1. **[COMPANY NAME]**, a [STATE] [corporation / limited liability company] with its principal place of business at [ADDRESS] (the "Company"); and
2. **[EXECUTIVE NAME]**, an individual residing at [ADDRESS] (the "Executive").

## 1. Position and duties

1.1 **Position.** The Company employs the Executive as its [TITLE], reporting to the [Board of Directors / Chief Executive Officer] (the "Board"). The Executive will have the duties, authority and responsibilities customary for that position and such other duties consistent with it as the Board assigns.

1.2 **Full time and attention.** The Executive will devote substantially all of their business time and attention to the Company. The Executive may (a) serve on the boards of [up to [NUMBER]] non-competing companies or non-profit organisations with the Board's prior written consent, (b) engage in civic and charitable activities, and (c) manage personal investments, in each case so long as those activities do not interfere with the Executive's duties or breach Section 9.

1.3 **Place of work.** The Executive's principal place of work is [the Company's offices at [ADDRESS] / the Executive's home office in [CITY, STATE]], with travel as reasonably required.

## 2. Term

2.1 The Executive's employment under this Agreement begins on [START DATE] (the "Start Date") and continues until terminated by either party under Section 7 (the "Term"). [Employment is at will.]

## 3. Compensation

3.1 **Base salary.** The Company will pay the Executive a base salary of $[AMOUNT] per year (the "Base Salary"), paid in accordance with the Company's regular payroll practices and subject to applicable withholding. The Board (or its compensation committee) will review the Base Salary at least annually and may increase but not decrease it [other than as part of an across-the-board reduction applying to all senior executives].

3.2 **Annual bonus.** For each fiscal year of the Term, the Executive is eligible for an annual cash bonus with a target of [PERCENT]% of Base Salary (the "Target Bonus"), based on performance goals set by the Board [in consultation with the Executive] within the first [90] days of the year. Any bonus earned will be paid when bonuses are paid to other senior executives and in any event by [March 15] of the following year. [Except as provided in Section 8, the Executive must be employed on the payment date to receive the bonus.]

3.3 **Sign-on bonus.** [The Company will pay the Executive a one-time sign-on bonus of $[AMOUNT] within [30] days after the Start Date. If the Executive resigns without Good Reason or is terminated for Cause within [12] months after the Start Date, the Executive will repay the [full / pro-rated] sign-on bonus within [30] days after the termination date.]

## 4. Equity

4.1 Subject to approval by the Board, the Company will grant the Executive [an option to purchase [NUMBER] shares of the Company's common stock / [NUMBER] restricted stock units] under the Company's [EQUITY PLAN NAME] (the "Plan"). The award will vest over [four] years, with [25]% vesting on the first anniversary of the Start Date and the remainder in equal [monthly / quarterly] instalments thereafter, subject to continued service, and will otherwise be governed by the Plan and the award agreement.

4.2 **Acceleration.** [If a Change of Control occurs and, within [12] months after it, the Executive's employment is terminated by the Company without Cause or by the Executive for Good Reason, [100]% of the then-unvested portion of the award will vest on the termination date.]

## 5. Benefits and expenses

5.1 The Executive may participate in the employee benefit plans the Company makes available to its senior executives from time to time, subject to the terms of those plans.

5.2 The Executive is entitled to [NUMBER] days of paid vacation per year [in addition to Company holidays], accruing and carried over in accordance with Company policy.

5.3 The Company will reimburse the Executive for reasonable business expenses incurred in performing their duties, in accordance with the Company's expense policy.

5.4 The Company will [pay / reimburse] the Executive's reasonable legal fees, up to $[AMOUNT], for the review and negotiation of this Agreement.

## 6. Indemnification and insurance

The Company will indemnify the Executive to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law and the Company's governing documents, and will cover the Executive under its directors' and officers' liability insurance on terms no less favourable than those applying to any other officer or director, during the Term and for [six] years afterwards.

## 7. Termination

7.1 **By the Company for Cause.** The Company may terminate the Executive's employment for Cause immediately on written notice. "**Cause**" means the Executive's (a) wilful and continued failure to perform material duties after written notice and a [30]-day cure period; (b) wilful misconduct or gross negligence that causes material harm to the Company; (c) conviction of, or plea of guilty or no contest to, a felony or a crime involving fraud or dishonesty; (d) material breach of this Agreement or of a written Company policy that, if curable, is not cured within [30] days after written notice; or (e) fraud, embezzlement or misappropriation against the Company.

7.2 **By the Company without Cause.** The Company may terminate the Executive's employment without Cause on [30] days' written notice [or pay in lieu of notice].

7.3 **By the Executive for Good Reason.** The Executive may resign for Good Reason. "**Good Reason**" means, without the Executive's written consent: (a) a material reduction in Base Salary or Target Bonus [other than as permitted by Section 3.1]; (b) a material diminution in the Executive's title, authority, duties or reporting line; (c) relocation of the Executive's principal place of work by more than [50] miles; or (d) a material breach of this Agreement by the Company; provided that the Executive gives written notice within [60] days after the condition first arises, the Company fails to cure it within [30] days after that notice, and the Executive resigns within [30] days after the cure period ends.

7.4 **By the Executive without Good Reason.** The Executive may resign without Good Reason on [60] days' written notice. The Company may waive all or part of the notice period [and pay Base Salary through the end of it].

7.5 **Death or Disability.** Employment ends automatically on the Executive's death. The Company may terminate employment on written notice if the Executive is unable, with reasonable accommodation, to perform the essential functions of the position for [120] consecutive days or [180] days in any 12-month period ("**Disability**"), subject to applicable law.

7.6 **Resignation from offices.** On termination for any reason, the Executive will be deemed to have resigned from every office and directorship held with the Company and its affiliates, and will sign any document reasonably requested to give effect to this.

## 8. Payments on termination

8.1 **Accrued obligations.** On any termination, the Company will pay the Executive (a) Base Salary earned through the termination date, (b) accrued but unused vacation to the extent required by law or Company policy, (c) unreimbursed business expenses, and (d) vested benefits under Company plans (together, the "**Accrued Obligations**").

8.2 **Severance.** If the Company terminates the Executive's employment without Cause (other than for death or Disability) or the Executive resigns for Good Reason, then, subject to Section 8.4, the Company will provide:

(a) continued payment of Base Salary for [12] months after the termination date (the "**Severance Period**"), paid on the Company's regular payroll schedule;

(b) [a pro-rated Target Bonus for the year of termination / any earned but unpaid bonus for the prior year], paid when bonuses are paid to other executives;

(c) if the Executive timely elects COBRA continuation coverage, payment or reimbursement of the employer portion of the premiums for the Executive and their covered dependants until the earlier of the end of the Severance Period and the date the Executive becomes eligible for coverage under another employer's plan; and

(d) [the acceleration in Section 4.2, where applicable].

8.3 **Change of Control.** [If the termination in Section 8.2 occurs within [three] months before or [12] months after a Change of Control, the Severance Period is [18] months and the bonus in Section 8.2(b) is [1.5] times the Target Bonus, paid in a lump sum.] "**Change of Control**" has the meaning given in the Plan.

8.4 **Release.** Severance under Sections 8.2 and 8.3 is conditional on the Executive signing, and not revoking, a separation agreement and general release in a form reasonably acceptable to the Company within [45] days after the termination date, and on the Executive's continued compliance with Sections 9 and 10. Payments that would otherwise be made before the release becomes effective will be paid in a lump sum on the first payroll date after it does.

8.5 **Section 409A.** This Agreement is intended to comply with, or be exempt from, Section 409A of the Internal Revenue Code and will be interpreted accordingly. Each instalment is a separate payment. If the Executive is a "specified employee" on the termination date, any payment of deferred compensation that would otherwise be made within six months after separation from service will instead be paid on the first business day after that six-month period (or on death, if earlier). Reimbursements will be made no later than the end of the year following the year the expense was incurred.

8.6 **Section 280G.** [If any payment would be subject to the excise tax under Section 4999 of the Internal Revenue Code, the payments will be reduced to the extent necessary to avoid the tax, but only if the reduction leaves the Executive in a better after-tax position than receiving the full payments and paying the tax.]

## 9. Restrictive covenants

> **Drafting note:** Check enforceability in the governing state before using this section. Several states ban or cap non-competes for employees, require advance notice, or impose minimum salary thresholds. Delete 9.2 where non-competes are void.

9.1 **Acknowledgement.** The Executive acknowledges that their position gives them access to Confidential Information and customer and employee relationships, and that the covenants below are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests.

9.2 **Non-competition.** During employment and for [12] months afterwards (the "**Restricted Period**"), the Executive will not, within [TERRITORY], directly or indirectly own, manage, operate, control, be employed by, or provide services to any business that competes with the Business. "**Business**" means [DESCRIPTION OF THE COMPANY'S BUSINESS]. Passive ownership of up to [2]% of a publicly traded company is permitted.

9.3 **Non-solicitation of customers.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive will not solicit or attempt to solicit, for a competing business, any customer or prospective customer of the Company with whom the Executive had material contact or about whom the Executive obtained Confidential Information in the last [24] months of employment.

9.4 **Non-solicitation of employees.** During the Restricted Period, the Executive will not solicit, recruit or hire, or attempt to solicit, recruit or hire, any employee or contractor of the Company, or induce any of them to leave the Company. General advertisements not targeted at Company personnel are not a breach.

9.5 **Non-disparagement.** The Executive will not make any statement that disparages the Company, its products, or its officers, directors or employees. The Company will instruct its directors and officers not to disparage the Executive. Nothing in this Section prevents truthful statements required by law or made to a government agency.

9.6 **Reformation.** If a court finds any covenant in this Section unenforceable as written, the court may modify it to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable.

## 10. Confidentiality and intellectual property

The Executive will sign the Company's standard [Confidential Information and Invention Assignment Agreement] as a condition of employment, and that agreement is incorporated into this Agreement. Nothing in this Agreement prohibits the Executive from reporting possible violations of law to a government agency, making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws, or disclosing trade secrets as permitted by 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b).

## 11. Clawback

Incentive compensation paid to the Executive is subject to recovery under any clawback policy the Company adopts to comply with applicable law or stock exchange listing standards, and to any other clawback policy applicable to senior executives generally.

## 12. General

12.1 **Governing law and venue.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [STATE], without regard to conflict-of-law rules. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in [COUNTY, STATE]. [Any dispute arising out of this Agreement will be resolved by binding arbitration administered by [JAMS / AAA] under its employment arbitration rules, except that either party may seek injunctive relief in court to enforce Sections 9 and 10.]

12.2 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement, together with the agreements referred to in Sections 4 and 10, is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter and supersedes all prior offers, agreements and understandings, written or oral.

12.3 **Amendment and waiver.** This Agreement may be amended only in writing signed by both parties. No waiver is effective unless in writing, and no waiver of one breach is a waiver of any other.

12.4 **Assignment.** The Company may assign this Agreement to a successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets, and will require the successor to assume it. The Executive may not assign this Agreement.

12.5 **Notices.** Notices must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognised courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses above (or any address later notified in writing).

12.6 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, the rest of this Agreement remains in effect.

12.7 **Withholding.** All payments are subject to applicable tax withholding.

12.8 **Survival.** Sections 6 and 8 to 12 survive termination of employment.

12.9 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including electronically, each of which is an original.

**Signed** by the parties on the date first written above.
