# Sublease Agreement (template)

For a tenant ("Sublessor") renting the premises to a subtenant ("Sublessee"). Check that the original lease **allows subletting** first. Replace every `[BRACKETED]` value. **Not legal advice — see [DISCLAIMER](../DISCLAIMER.md).**

---

**SUBLEASE AGREEMENT**

Made on `[DATE]` between `[ORIGINAL TENANT NAME]` ("Sublessor") and `[SUBTENANT NAME]` ("Sublessee") for `[PROPERTY ADDRESS]`.

**1. Master lease.** This Sublease is subject to the original lease dated `[DATE]` between Sublessor and `[LANDLORD]`. Landlord consent: `[attached / obtained on DATE]`.

**2. Term.** `[START]` to `[END]` (cannot exceed the master lease term).

**3. Rent.** `$[AMOUNT]`/month, due `[DAY]`, paid to `[Sublessor]`.

**4. Deposit.** `$[AMOUNT]`.

**5. Obligations.** Sublessee follows all terms of the master lease (attached). Sublessor remains responsible to Landlord.

**6. Governing law.** Laws of `[STATE]`.
