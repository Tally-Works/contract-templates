# Property Management Agreement (template)

Between a property owner and a property manager/management company. Replace every `[BRACKETED]` value. **Not legal advice — see [DISCLAIMER](../DISCLAIMER.md).**

---

**PROPERTY MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT**

Made on `[DATE]` between `[OWNER NAME]` ("Owner") and `[MANAGER / COMPANY NAME]` ("Manager") for the property at `[ADDRESS]`.

**1. Appointment.** Owner appoints Manager to manage the property.

**2. Term.** `[START]` to `[END]`, `[renewal / termination notice]`.

**3. Manager duties.** `[Marketing & leasing, tenant screening, rent collection, maintenance coordination, inspections, accounting/reporting, handling notices]`.

**4. Management fee.** `[% of monthly rent / flat fee]`; leasing fee: `[amount]`; other fees: `[list]`.

**5. Owner responsibilities.** `[Funding a reserve, approving expenses over $X, insurance, mortgage/taxes]`.

**6. Authority & limits.** Manager may incur repairs up to `$[LIMIT]` without prior approval; above that requires Owner consent.

**7. Trust account.** Rents and deposits held in `[trust/escrow account]` per applicable law.

**8. Insurance & indemnity.** `[Coverage, indemnity terms]`.

**9. Governing law.** Laws of `[STATE]`.
