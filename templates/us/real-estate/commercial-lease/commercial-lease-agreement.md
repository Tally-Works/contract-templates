# Commercial Lease Agreement (template)

For leasing office, retail, or industrial space. Commercial leases are more negotiable and higher-stakes than residential — **have a lawyer review before signing.** Replace every `[BRACKETED]` value. **Not legal advice — see [DISCLAIMER](../DISCLAIMER.md).**

---

**COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT**

Made on `[DATE]` between `[LANDLORD NAME]` ("Landlord") and `[TENANT / BUSINESS NAME]` ("Tenant") for the premises at `[ADDRESS, SUITE, SQ FT]` (the "Premises").

**1. Permitted use.** Tenant may use the Premises only for `[PERMITTED USE]`.

**2. Term.** `[START]` to `[END]`, with `[renewal option(s)]`.

**3. Rent.** Base rent `$[AMOUNT]`/month. Rent escalations: `[annual % or schedule]`.

**4. Lease type.** `[Gross / Net / NNN — specify who pays taxes, insurance, CAM/common-area maintenance]`.

**5. Security deposit.** `$[AMOUNT]`.

**6. Improvements.** `[Tenant improvement allowance / who builds out / restoration at end]`.

**7. Maintenance & repairs.** `[Allocation between Landlord and Tenant]`.

**8. Insurance & indemnity.** Tenant carries `[coverage types/limits]` and names Landlord as additional insured.

**9. Assignment / sublease.** `[Allowed with consent / not allowed]`. See [sublease agreement](sublease-agreement.md).

**10. Default & remedies.** `[Cure periods, remedies]`.

**11. Governing law.** Laws of `[STATE]`.
