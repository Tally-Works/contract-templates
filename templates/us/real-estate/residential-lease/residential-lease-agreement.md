# Residential Lease Agreement (template)

A standard fixed-term residential lease between a landlord and tenant. Replace every `[BRACKETED]` value, adjust clauses for your state, and send for e-signature. **Not legal advice — see [DISCLAIMER](../DISCLAIMER.md).**

---

**RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT**

This Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made on `[DATE]` between `[LANDLORD NAME]` ("Landlord") and `[TENANT NAME(S)]` ("Tenant").

**1. Property.** Landlord rents to Tenant the residential property at `[PROPERTY ADDRESS]` (the "Premises").

**2. Term.** The Lease begins on `[START DATE]` and ends on `[END DATE]` (fixed term).

**3. Rent.** Tenant pays `$[AMOUNT]` per month, due on the `[DAY]` of each month, to `[PAYMENT METHOD/ADDRESS]`. Late rent after `[GRACE PERIOD]` days incurs a fee of `$[LATE FEE]`.

**4. Security deposit.** Tenant pays a deposit of `$[AMOUNT]`, returned within `[# DAYS — check your state limit]` days after move-out, less lawful deductions.

**5. Occupancy.** The Premises are for residential use by `[NAMED OCCUPANTS]` only.

**6. Utilities.** Tenant is responsible for: `[LIST]`. Landlord is responsible for: `[LIST]`.

**7. Maintenance.** Tenant keeps the Premises clean and reports needed repairs. Landlord maintains the Premises in habitable condition as required by law.

**8. Pets.** `[Allowed / Not allowed]`. If allowed: `[pet deposit / terms]`.

**9. Entry.** Landlord may enter with `[NOTICE PERIOD — check your state]` notice, except in emergencies.

**10. Termination & renewal.** `[Auto month-to-month after term / renewal terms]`. Early termination: `[terms]`.

**11. Required disclosures.** `[Insert state/federal disclosures — e.g. lead-paint disclosure for pre-1978 housing, security-deposit terms, etc. See ../state-notes/state-considerations.md]`

**12. Governing law.** This Lease is governed by the laws of `[STATE]`.
