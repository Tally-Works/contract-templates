# Notice to Vacate (template)

A written notice ending a tenancy. Can be sent by a landlord or tenant. Required notice periods and grounds **vary by state** — check yours. Replace every `[BRACKETED]` value. **Not legal advice — see [DISCLAIMER](../DISCLAIMER.md).**

---

**NOTICE TO VACATE**

Date: `[DATE]`

To: `[RECIPIENT NAME]`  
Property: `[PROPERTY ADDRESS]`

This letter serves as formal notice that the tenancy at the above property will end on **`[MOVE-OUT DATE]`**, which is at least `[# DAYS — check your state]` days from the date of this notice.

`[If landlord and grounds are required, state the reason and cite the applicable notice type — e.g. end of term, non-payment, lease violation. Some states require specific language and forms.]`

Please ensure the property is vacated and returned in good condition, keys returned to `[LOCATION]`, and a forwarding address provided for the security-deposit return.

Sincerely,  
`[SENDER NAME]`  
`[CONTACT]`

Signature: __________________ Date: ______
