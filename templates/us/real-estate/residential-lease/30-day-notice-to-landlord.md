# 30-Day Notice to Landlord (template)

A tenant's written notice to end a month-to-month tenancy and move out. Confirm your required notice period (often 30 days, but **varies by state and lease**). Replace every `[BRACKETED]` value. **Not legal advice — see [DISCLAIMER](../DISCLAIMER.md).**

---

**30-DAY NOTICE TO LANDLORD**

Date: `[DATE]`

To: `[LANDLORD / PROPERTY MANAGER NAME]`  
Re: `[PROPERTY ADDRESS, UNIT]`

Dear `[LANDLORD NAME]`,

This is my written notice that I will vacate the above property on **`[MOVE-OUT DATE]`**, at least 30 days from today, ending my tenancy.

Please send my security-deposit refund to my forwarding address: `[FORWARDING ADDRESS]`. I will return keys to `[LOCATION]` and leave the unit clean and undamaged. Let me know a convenient time for a move-out inspection.

Thank you,  
`[TENANT NAME]`  
`[CONTACT]`

Signature: __________________ Date: ______
