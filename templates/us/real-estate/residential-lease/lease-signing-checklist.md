# Lease signing checklist

Confirm these before both parties sign — it prevents the most common lease disputes.

## Before signing
- [ ] Legal names of all parties are correct and spelled right.
- [ ] Property address (and unit) is exact.
- [ ] Term dates (start/end) are correct.
- [ ] Rent amount, due date, method, and late fee are stated.
- [ ] Security deposit amount and return timeline match your **state's legal limit**.
- [ ] Utilities responsibility is split explicitly.
- [ ] Pet, smoking, guest, and parking terms are included.
- [ ] Entry-notice period matches your state's requirement.
- [ ] **Required disclosures** are attached (e.g. lead-paint for pre-1978 housing; state-specific disclosures — see [state notes](../state-notes/state-considerations.md)).
- [ ] Any handwritten changes are initialed by both parties.

## Signatures
- [ ] Every named tenant signs (not just one).
- [ ] Landlord/authorized agent signs.
- [ ] Each party gets a fully signed copy.
- [ ] If signing electronically, each signer is verified by unique email and the signed PDF + audit trail is retained. See the [lease → signature workflow](../workflows/lease-to-signature.md).
