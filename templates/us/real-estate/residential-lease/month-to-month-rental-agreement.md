# Month-to-Month Rental Agreement (template)

**MONTH-TO-MONTH RENTAL AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is made on `[DATE]` between `[LANDLORD NAME]` ("Landlord") and `[TENANT NAME(S)]` ("Tenant") for the property at `[PROPERTY ADDRESS]` (the "Premises").

**1. Term.** The tenancy begins `[START DATE]` and continues **month to month** until terminated as below.

**2. Rent.** `$[AMOUNT]` per month, due on the `[DAY]` of each month.

**3. Termination.** Either party may end this Agreement by giving at least `[# DAYS — commonly 30; check your state]` written notice. See [notice to vacate](notice-to-vacate.md).

**4. Rent changes.** Landlord may change rent with `[# DAYS]` written notice, subject to local rent-control rules.

**5. Security deposit.** `$[AMOUNT]`, returned within `[# DAYS]` of move-out less lawful deductions.
