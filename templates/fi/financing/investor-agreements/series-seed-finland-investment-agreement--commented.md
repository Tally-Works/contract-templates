1.  COMPENSATION AND LIMITATIONS OF LIABILITY
    1.  General

The Investors are entering into this Agreement in reliance upon each of the Representations and Warranties under Section 5 being true, correct and not misleading on the Effective Date. The Warrantors undertake to indemnify and compensate the Investors severally but not jointly for any direct losses and any reasonably foreseeable (as of the Effective Date) indirect losses the Investors may suffer, including any damage occurring to the Company (in which case each Investor's Loss shall be determined in accordance with Section 6.1.3), should any of the Representations and Warranties turn out to be untrue, incorrect or misleading on the Effective Date (a **Loss**).

Unless a breach of the Representations and Warranties is attributable to fraud or gross negligence on the part of a Founder, in which case the Investors shall be entitled to claim compensation directly from such Founder without first seeking recovery from the Company, the Company's liability under this Section 6 shall be primary and the Founders' liability shall be secondary. Accordingly, the Investors shall first seek recovery from the Company in respect of any Losses, and the Founders shall have no liability in respect of any Losses unless and until (a) a valid claim has been notified to the Company in accordance with this Section 6, (b) the Company has failed to satisfy such Losses in full within [30] Business Days of such notification, and (c) the Investors have taken all reasonable steps to enforce and recover such Losses from the Company. The liability of each Founder under this Section 6 shall in all cases be subject to the limitations set out in this Section 6.

If such damage has occurred to the Company, each Investor shall be deemed to have incurred damage equalling the total amount of the damage multiplied by such Investor’s fully-diluted ownership percentage in the Company.

-   1.  Limitations of Liability

The Warrantors’ total liability to the Investors for a breach of the Representations and Warranties shall not exceed the amount invested pursuant to this Agreement. The Company’s liability to an Investor for a breach of the Representations and Warranties shall not exceed the amount invested by such Investor pursuant to this Agreement, and the Founders’ aggregate liability to an Investor for a breach of the Representations and Warranties on a pro rata basis shall not exceed [30]% of the amount invested by such Investor pursuant to this Agreement. For the avoidance of doubt, the Founders' liability under this Section 6.2.1 shall only arise to the extent that the Company has failed to satisfy the relevant Losses in accordance with Section 6.1.2. The aggregate compensation to an Investor shall not exceed the amount invested by such Investor pursuant to this Agreement.

The Warrantors shall not be liable in respect of any individual claim for breach of the Representations and Warranties where the amount of such claim is less than EUR [5,000], and such claim shall not be taken into account for the purposes of calculating the basket referred to below. The Warrantors shall not be liable in respect of any claim or claims for breach of the Representations and Warranties unless the aggregate amount of all such claims (excluding claims below the de minimis threshold) exceeds EUR [20,000], in which case the Warrantors shall be liable for the full amount of such claims and not merely the excess over such threshold. Any claim for a breach of the Representations and Warranties set out in Section 5 shall be made within eighteen (18) months of the Effective Date. No limitations of liability set out in this Agreement shall apply to intentional or grossly negligent breaches of the Representations and Warranties.

The Warrantors’ Representations and Warranties under Section 5 are the sole and exclusive representations and warranties, and no Party may rely on any other representations, warranties or guarantees whatsoever, whether implied or arising out of the applicable legislation, unless expressly set out in this Section 6. For the avoidance of doubt, the Company and/or the Founders may not rely on any representations or warranties whatsoever given by the Investors.

Except for the Representations and Warranties set out in Sections 5.2 and 5.3, all of the Representations and Warranties shall be qualified by any fact, matter, event, circumstance or other matter fairly disclosed in the Disclosure Letter. Accordingly, except for the Representations and Warranties set out in Sections 5.2 and 5.3, the Company and the Founders shall not be deemed to be in breach of the Representations and Warranties to the extent a fact, matter, event or circumstance is fairly disclosed to the Investors in the Disclosure Letter.

-   1.  Compensatory Issue

Should the Company be liable to compensate an Investor under this Section 6, such Investor shall, at its sole discretion, be entitled to request such compensation to be paid, in whole or in part, by way of issuance of new Series Seed Shares for subscription by such Investor in accordance with this Section 6.3 (the **Compensatory Issue**).

The number of new Series Seed Shares to be issued in a Compensatory Issue shall be calculated by dividing the amount to be compensated by the Company to such Investor in the form of Series Seed Shares (the **Loss Amount**) by a price per Series Seed Share, which shall be calculated by subtracting the Loss Amount from the pre-money valuation of the Company used in the Investment and dividing the result by the total number of shares in the Company on a fully diluted basis immediately prior to the Investment. The subscription price payable by the relevant Investor in the Compensatory Issue shall be set off against the Loss Amount payable by the Company to such Investor.

-   1.  Compensatory Sale

Such Common Shares shall be converted into Series Seed Shares simultaneously with such transfer. The number of Shares to be transferred and the price to be used shall be determined following the principles set out in Section 6.3.2.

Each Party undertakes to execute, make and do all such resolutions, documents and acts as may be necessary to effectively complete a Compensatory Issue and a Compensatory Sale in accordance with Sections 6.3 and 6.4.
