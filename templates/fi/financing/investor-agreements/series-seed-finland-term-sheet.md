**TERM SHEET**

**[Date of the Term Sheet]**

This term sheet (the **Term Sheet**) outlines the principal terms and conditions of a proposed investment by [Investor] in [Company] (the **Investment**).

**TARGET COMPANY**
[●], a Finnish limited liability company with business identity code [Business ID] (the **Company**)

**INVESTOR(S)**
[Investor] (the **Investor**)
[Co-investor] (the **Co-investor**)

**FOUNDERS**
[Company/Personal Names, Company/Personal ID Numbers/Dates of Birth] (together the **Founders**). In this Term Sheet, “Founder” may refer to a company and/or the physical person in control of such a company.

**DOCUMENTATION**
The Parties shall negotiate and enter into a shareholders’ agreement relating to the Company [superseding and replacing any previous shareholders’ agreement relating to the Company], an investment agreement relating to the issuance of new preferred series seed shares in the Company to the Investor(s), and other related agreements to be executed by the Parties and/or shareholders in connection with the Investment (the **Investment Documents**).

**INVESTMENT INSTRUMENT**
New preferred series seed shares in the Company (the **Series Seed Shares**)

**VALUATION**
Fully-diluted pre-money valuation of the Company immediately prior to the closing of the Investment (the **Closing**) is EUR [●]. Subscription price per Series Seed Share is EUR [●].
[The pre-money valuation includes an option pool representing [●]% of the fully diluted ownership of the Company (the **ESOP**).]

**INVESTMENT AMOUNT**
The Investor will invest EUR [●].
[The Co-investor will invest EUR [●].]
[In addition, the existing shareholders will invest EUR [●].]
The fully diluted capitalization of the Company immediately before and after the Closing is set out in Schedule 1.

**LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE**
In the event of a liquidation, dissolution or winding up of the Company, or a merger, sale or other type of transaction in which control in the Company or substantially all of its assets are transferred, the holders of Series Seed Shares shall receive, per Series Seed Share, the higher of (i) one times the original subscription price paid for such Series Seed Share; or (ii) the amount they would receive if all shareholders received their pro rata share of such assets or proceeds. The remaining proceeds shall be distributed to the holders of common shares on a pro rata basis.

**CONVERSION**
The Series Seed Shares shall be convertible to common shares at any time at the option of the holder. The conversion rate shall be 1:1, subject to customary adjustments for share splits, share dividends, combinations, recapitalisations, etc.

**BOARD**
The board shall consist of a maximum of [five (5)] members. [Two (2)] members shall be elected by the holders of the majority of the common shares and [one (1)] member shall be elected by the holders of the majority of the Series Seed Shares.

**INFORMATION AND INSPECTION RIGHTS**
The Investor(s) shall receive customary information and inspection rights.

**PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS**
Prior written consent of the holders of the majority of the Series Seed Shares will be required to make any of the following decisions:
(a) adversely change the rights of the Series Seed Shares or amend the articles of association;
(b) issue, redeem, purchase or otherwise acquire, or sell or otherwise transfer any Shares [other than in connection with the ESOP or pursuant to a compensatory issue under the investment agreement];
(c) declare or pay any dividend or make a decision on any other asset distribution;
(d) change the number of the board members;
(e) merge, demerge, liquidate or dissolve the Company, including any liquidation event;
(f) transfer, lease, license (other than licenses granted in the ordinary course of business on a non-exclusive basis), pledge or encumber assets or rights material to the Company’s business;
(g) enter into any agreement or assignment with a shareholder or its immediate family member or any entity controlled by a shareholder and/or its immediate family member or make any amendments thereto; or
(h) hire or fire any Working Shareholders (as defined below) or amend the terms of service or employment agreements of the Working Shareholders.

**PRE-EMPTIVE RIGHT**
Each holder of the Series Seed Shares shall have the right to participate in new securities that the Company may from time to time propose to sell or issue. The exercising holders will also have an overallotment right if any other holder does not exercise its pre-emptive right.

**DRAG ALONG, RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL AND TAG ALONG RIGHTS**
The Investment Documents shall contain customary drag-along, tag-along and right of first refusal provisions to the satisfaction of the Investor.

**DRAG-ALONG**
If the shareholders holding at least two thirds of the shares held by all shareholders, including always the shareholders holding more than 50% of the Series Seed Shares, wishes to sell their shares or carry out the sale of the Company’s business or any other exit, they have the right to drag the rest of the shareholders in the Company in such a transaction.

**TAG-ALONG**
The shareholders shall have the right to participate in any sale or other transfer of shares in the same proportion and on the same terms and conditions as offered to the selling shareholder.

**RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL**
Transfer of shares in the Company is subject to the Company’s and the other shareholders’ right of first refusal.

**RESTRICTIONS ON TRANSFERS**
Before exit, no shareholder shall transfer any shares unless such transfer is expressly permitted under the Investment Documents.

**WORKING COMMITMENT AND VESTING**
The Founders [and certain key employees] (together, the Working Shareholders) shall, subject to permitted secondary occupations listed in a schedule to the shareholders' agreement, use 100% of their business hours for the benefit of the Company or its subsidiaries.
The Founders’ [and other Working Shareholders'] common shares shall be subject to a four (4) year vesting period as follows: twenty-five per cent (25%) of the common shares shall vest one (1) year following the vesting commencement date (the cliff), with the remaining seventy-five per cent (75%) vesting in equal monthly instalments over the following three (3) years. Shares subscribed for with stock options under the ESOP shall not be subject to the vesting and purchase right provisions.
If the employment or service relationship of a Working Shareholder terminates, the Company primarily and the other shareholders (excluding the departing Working Shareholder) secondarily on a pro rata basis shall have a right to purchase all or part of the common shares of such Working Shareholder, subject to the following leaver classifications and pricing:
**Bad Leaver**
**Early Leaver**
**Good Leaver**
**Shares subject to purchase**
All Common Shares (vested and unvested)
All Common Shares (vested and unvested)
Unvested Common Shares only
**Price – Unvested Shares**
Lower of (i) original purchase/subscription price; or (ii) 25% of Fair Market Value
Lower of (i) original purchase/subscription price; or (ii) Fair Market Value
Higher of (i) original purchase/subscription price; or (ii) Fair Market Value
**Price – Vested Shares**
Higher of (i) original purchase/subscription price; or (ii) 25% of Fair Market Value
Higher of (i) original purchase/subscription price; or (ii) Fair Market Value
N/A – vested Common Shares are not subject to purchase rights
**Retained Shares**
None – all Common Shares may be purchased
None – all Common Shares may be purchased
Vested Common Shares are retained; such shares may be sold to any Shareholder, subject to the right of first refusal provisions

**DUE DILIGENCE**
The Company shall assist, and shall procure that its advisors will assist, the Investor in conducting due diligence for evaluating this investment and shall fully disclose and not withhold information which is necessary for the due diligence process.

**EXPENSES**
Each Party shall bear their own costs and expenses incurred in connection with preparation of the Investment and the Investment Documents. However, all reasonable out-of-pocket costs and expenses (including legal fees and due diligence expenses) incurred by the Investor [up to EUR ● (excluding VAT)] shall be reimbursed by the Company.

**TARGET CLOSING DATE**
[Target Closing Date]

**EXCLUSIVITY**
During the term of this Term Sheet, the Company and the Founders undertake not to (i) enter into, continue, or encourage discussions or proposals concerning transfer of the shares (or any part thereof) or material assets or business of the Company with any third party nor enter into agreements related thereto, (ii) enter into, continue, or encourage discussions or proposals concerning arrangements or agreements which could change the capital structure or the ownership of the Company, or prevent or have other impact on the effective outcome of the Investment nor enter into agreements related thereto, or (iii) provide or agree to provide any incentives for the members of the Board or key employees of the Company triggered as a result of the Investment.

**CONFIDENTIALITY**
All parties shall treat the content and the existence of this Term Sheet as confidential information (**Confidential Information**), and without the written consents of the other parties, no party shall disclose any Confidential Information to any person, or make or allow to make any announcement regarding any Confidential Information in a press release, publication or others to the public, except for disclosure made to their respective directors, employees or professional advisers on a need-to-know basis, and other than as required by applicable laws, regulations and listing rules of relevant stock exchanges.

**GOVERNING LAW**
This Term Sheet is governed by the laws of Finland without regard to its principles and rules on conflict of laws.
Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this contract, or the breach, termination or validity thereof, shall be finally settled by arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration Rules of the Finland Chamber of Commerce. The number of arbitrators shall be one (1). The seat of arbitration shall be Helsinki, Finland. The language of the proceedings shall be [Finnish/English]. However, evidence may be submitted and witnesses may be heard in [Finnish/English], if the arbitral tribunal deems it appropriate.

**COUNTERPARTS**
This Term Sheet may be executed in any number of counterparts and all of the counterparts taken together shall be deemed to constitute one and the same instrument. Electronic signature or delivery of an executed counterpart of a signature page to this Term Sheet by email shall be effective as delivery of a manually executed counterpart of this Term Sheet.

**INDICATIVE TERMS**
This Term Sheet is only intended to be a statement of intentions and nothing in this Term Sheet creates any obligation on the Parties to carry out the Investment or enter into the Investment Documents. Except for the “Expenses,” “Exclusivity,” “Confidentiality,” “Governing Law,” and “Counterparts” provisions of this Term Sheet, which provisions shall be binding, this Term Sheet is not intended to be construed as a binding agreement with respect of the subject matter hereof.
