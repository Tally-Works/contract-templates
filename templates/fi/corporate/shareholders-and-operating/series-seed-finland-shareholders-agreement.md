1.  BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE

Upon entering into this Agreement, the Parties agree on certain rights and obligations of the Shareholders and the Company, as well as on the management of the Company.

On the Effective Date, the Company is [and its Subsidiaries are] engaged in the business of [specify scope of business] (the **Business**).

On the even date herewith, the Parties have executed an investment agreement relating to the Company (the **Investment Agreement**).

The objective of the Parties is to manage and develop the Business and the operations of the Company in order to maximise the value of the Shares. The Parties also intend to raise additional funds for the Company. The Parties acknowledge and understand that a new investor who will provide such funds might require the Shareholders to enter into a new shareholders’ agreement and that the new investor may require that the shares issued to it shall be preferred shares.

The Parties may also enter into a minority shareholders’ agreement to which certain minority shareholders and/or transferees of Shares are required to adhere as decided by the Board. In the event of any discrepancy between this Agreement and the possible minority shareholders’ agreement, this Agreement shall prevail.

The Shareholders warrant that they have carefully and independently evaluated the rights and obligations arising out of this Agreement, as well as the risks and opportunities associated with it.

1.  GENERAL OBLIGATIONS

The Working Shareholders agree to contribute to the Company’s success and to enable it to achieve the objectives set forth in the business plan of the Company (as amended from time to time by the Board).

Each Shareholder undertakes, also through the members of the Board or other representatives appointed by it, at the Shareholders’ Meetings, at the meetings of the Board and on any other occasion to exercise its voting rights and to act otherwise in a way necessary to ensure the proper realization of and compliance with the terms of this Agreement and the Articles of Association. For the avoidance of doubt, the above does not mean that the Shareholder is required at all times to be present or represented in the Shareholders’ Meeting or at the meetings of the Board.

1.  CAPITALISATION AND SHARES
    1.  Common and Preferred Shares

The Company has two (2) separate classes of shares: common shares (the **Common Shares**) and preferred series seed shares (the **Series Seed Shares**). The Series Seed Shares shall have preference as set out in Section 5.2 over the Common Shares in the Liquidation Events and shall be convertible into Common Shares at any time as set out in Section 5.3 below but otherwise the Common Shares and the Series Seed Shares shall have equal rights in the Company. Each Common Share and Series Seed Share shall carry one (1) vote in a Shareholders’ Meeting.

-   1.  Liquidation Preference

In the event of any Liquidation Event of the Company, the proceeds shall be paid to the Shareholders as follows:

1.  First, the greater of:
2.  the original subscription price paid for each Series Seed Share added with declared but unpaid dividends on each Series Seed Share; or
3.  the amount that the holder of Series Seed Shares would have received, had the Series Seed Shares been converted into Common Shares immediately prior to the Liquidation Event

shall be paid to the holders of the Series Seed Shares.

1.  Secondly, the remaining proceeds shall be distributed to the holders of Common Shares on a pro rata basis.
    1.  Conversion

Each Series Seed Share shall be convertible into one (1) Common Share at any time upon the request of each holder of the Series Seed Shares, subject to adjustments for stock splits, reverse splits and the like.

-   1.  [Employee Stock Option Plan]

The Parties agree that stock options may be offered to the current or future key personnel of the Company (including outside expert members of the Board). For this purpose, the Company’s existing Shareholders have, on the date of this Agreement, made a resolution on the creation of the employee stock option plan (the **ESOP**) consisting of [10.0]% of all Shares (on a fully diluted basis) after the Closing (as defined in the Investment Agreement). The stock options issued under the ESOP shall entitle the holders to new Common Shares. The Investors and the Founders shall, subject to the prior written consent of the holders of the majority of the Series Seed Shares, agree on the structuring and other details of the ESOP.

The issuance of stock options or Shares pursuant to the ESOP shall always require the holder of stock options or Shares to adhere to this Agreement and/or to the possible minority shareholders’ agreement regarding the Company or an option agreement in accordance with Section 9.3.]

1.  FUTURE FINANCING
    1.  General

The Parties acknowledge and accept that the Company may from time to time issue new Shares strictly in accordance with this Agreement.

It is expressly agreed and understood that the Shareholders shall not, under this Agreement or otherwise, unless expressly otherwise agreed in writing on a case-by-case basis, be obligated to participate in any possible issue of Shares or grant a loan to the Company or any Subsidiary, if applicable, or to guarantee or provide any security for any debt or other liability of the Company or any Subsidiary.

All Investors shall have a pro rata right, but not an obligation, to participate in subsequent issuances of any Shares on the same terms and conditions as any other Shareholder or third-party investor or financer, provided that the pre-emptive right of the Investors shall not apply to any Exempted Issuance. In addition, should any Investor choose not to purchase or subscribe to its full pro rata portion (calculated based on the Investor’s then-current shareholding) in such issuances of any Shares, the other Investors shall have the right to purchase or subscribe to the remaining pro rata portion of the Shares. In the event an Investor has not replied or notified the Company in writing of its participation in the issuance of the Shares within fourteen (14) days after having received a notice by letter or email from the Company about the terms of the issuance, it shall be deemed to have irrevocably waived its subscription right to its pro rata part of issued Shares.

-   1.  Third-Party Financing

In the event that the Company is raising funds (such as through a third-party financer as mentioned under Section 6.1), the Shareholders understand that there may be a need to enter into a new shareholders’ agreement or other agreements (such as an investment agreement or a subscription agreement) regarding the Company and/or to amend the Articles of Association as well as to take other actions to secure the Company’s financing. Further, the Shareholders acknowledge that the Shares issued in such a financing round may be preferred shares with superior rights over the current Shares. Therefore, the Shareholders commit to vote in favour of any decisions in the Shareholders’ Meetings relating to amending the Articles of Association, directed Share issuances, other corporate resolutions on financing and any other matters required to complete the fund-raising, provided that the holders of more than 2/3 of the Series Seed Shares and the holders of more than 2/3 of the Common Shares vote for it. The Shareholders shall also sign and execute any shareholders’ agreement and other agreements (such as investment agreement or subscription agreement) required for completion of the above actions. If a Shareholder does not execute such agreement(s) or vote in favour of the above actions simultaneously with the other Shareholders, such Shareholder shall be deemed to have appointed the Board, or any person appointed by the Board, for that purpose to be its sole agent and attorney to execute all necessary agreements, to vote in favour of any matters and to take any other necessary actions on its behalf provided that if such agreements would impose any onerous obligations on a Shareholder adversely deviating from the obligations of other similarly situated Shareholders, the consent of the affected Shareholder to such agreement shall be specifically required.

1.  Purchase OF SHARES FROM WORKING SHAREHOLDERS
    1.  Working Commitment and Vesting

The Working Shareholders shall, subject to the permitted secondary occupations listed in the Schedule 2, use 100% of their business hours for the benefit of the Company or its Subsidiaries. The Common Shares owned by the Working Shareholders are subject to the purchase rights in accordance with the provisions of this Section 8.

If a Working Shareholder’s service or employment agreement is terminated, the Company primarily and the other Shareholders (other than the Working Shareholder whose service or employment agreement has been terminated) (the **Shareholders with Purchase Right**) secondarily on a pro rata basis to their then-current ownership of the Shares on a fully-diluted basis, have a right to purchase all or part of the Shares of such Working Shareholder against a cash payment as set forth in this Section 8.

The Common Shares of the Working Shareholders (including, for the sake of clarity, the Founders) shall vest as follows: 25% of the Common Shares shall vest one (1) year following the vesting commencement date with the remaining 75% of the Common Shares vesting in equal monthly instalments over the next three (3) years, thus one hundred percent (100%) of the Working Shareholder’s Common Shares will vest in four (4) years from the vesting commencement date.

The vesting commencement date shall mean (i) in respect of the Founders, the Effective Date, and (ii) in respect of the other Working Shareholders, the date of such Working Shareholder’s adherence to this Agreement. Notwithstanding anything to the contrary above, the Board shall have the right to agree on deviations to the above regular vesting periods on a case-by-case basis in writing, subject to the written consent of the Board member(s) appointed by the holders of the Series Seed Shares or, if such Board member has not been appointed, subject to the written consent of the holders of the majority of the Series Seed Shares.

In the case of employee stock options, the terms of the stock option scheme shall prevail, i.e. the Shares subscribed for with stock options shall not be subject to the vesting and purchase right provisions of this Section 8.

A Trade Sale shall accelerate the vesting of the Working Shareholder’s unvested Shares to the effect that all the Working Shareholder’s unvested Shares will vest upon the occurrence of a Trade Sale provided that, as a result of such Trade Sale, the respective Working Shareholder’s service or employment relationship is terminated.

-   1.  Bad Leaver, Early Leaver and Good Leaver

Irrespective of whether the Employment Contracts Act applies to the employment or service agreement of a Working Shareholder with the Company, if the employment or service relationship of a Working Shareholder terminates for any reason under circumstances where an employer would be entitled to:

1.  cancel an employment relationship pursuant to Section 1 of Chapter 8 of the Employment Contracts Act; or
2.  terminate the employment relationship pursuant to Section 2 of Chapter 7 of the Employment Contracts Act, excluding termination due to the death or permanent incapacity to work due to illness or injury;

such Working Shareholder shall be regarded as a **Bad Leaver.**

If the employment or service relationship of a Working Shareholder is cancelled or terminated by the respective Working Shareholder on his/her/their own initiative without a reason permitting cancellation or termination for reasons attributable to the employer pursuant to Section 1 of Chapter 8 of the Employment Contracts Act, such Working Shareholder shall be regarded as an **Early Leaver**. For the avoidance of doubt, if at the time of the Working Shareholder's resignation or termination on his/her/their own initiative, circumstances exist or existed that would have entitled the Company to cancel or terminate the Working Shareholder's employment or service relationship on grounds set out in Section 8.2.1, such Working Shareholder shall be regarded as a Bad Leaver notwithstanding the fact that the Working Shareholder resigned or terminated the relationship on his/her/their own initiative.

If the employment or service relationship of a Working Shareholder with the Company terminates for any other reason than the Bad Leaver reasons stated above in Section 8.2.1 or the Early Leaver reasons stated in Section 8.2.2, the respective Working Shareholder shall be regarded as a **Good Leaver**.

-   1.  Purchase of Shares

With respect to a Bad Leaver, all Common Shares may be purchased. The price for the Bad Leaver's unvested Common Shares to be purchased shall be the lower of (i) the original purchase price and/or subscription price of such Common Shares paid by the respective Working Shareholder; or (ii) 25% of the Fair Market Value of such Common Shares. The price for the Bad Leaver's vested Common Shares to be purchased shall be the higher of (i) the original purchase price and/or subscription price of such Common Shares paid by the respective Working Shareholder; or (ii) 25% of the Fair Market Value of such Common Shares.

With respect to an Early Leaver, all Common Shares may be purchased. The price for the Early Leaver's unvested Common Shares to be purchased shall be the lower of (i) the original purchase price and/or subscription price of such Common Shares paid by the respective Working Shareholder; or (ii) the Fair Market Value of such Common Shares. The price for the Early Leaver's vested Common Shares to be purchased shall be the higher of (i) the original purchase price and/or subscription price of such Common Shares paid by the respective Working Shareholder; or (ii) the Fair Market Value of such Common Shares.

With respect to a Good Leaver, only unvested Common Shares may be purchased. The price for the Good Leaver’s unvested Common Shares to be purchased shall be the higher of (i) the original purchase price and/or subscription price of such Common Shares paid by the respective Working Shareholder; or (ii) the Fair Market Value of such Common Shares. Any vested Common Shares of the Good Leaver that are not purchased pursuant to this Section 8.3.3 may be sold by the Good Leaver to any Shareholder, provided that the right of first refusal provisions set out in this Agreement shall apply to any such sale.

The termination of the Working Shareholder’s employment or service relationship with the Company shall be deemed to constitute an offer from such Working Shareholder primarily to the Company and secondarily to the Shareholders with Purchase Right to purchase all or part of the Working Shareholder’s applicable Common Shares.
